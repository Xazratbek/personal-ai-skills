# Spec ad: brief template and standing rules

A spec ad is a short (≈15–25 s + end card) brand film made without the brand commissioning it, or for a client who wants an
"Apple/Spotify/Shopify-like" finish. Write the brief to a file first and get an OK before spending credits.

## Brief file (`brief/PROMPT.md`)
- **Concept** — one sentence of message + the visual idea (e.g. "the order, the customer and the money are yours: real iPhone, real screens, kinetic type on a dark stage").
- **Beat sheet** — table: beat, time window, spoken line, picture. Times are provisional: final times come from the voice alignment.
- **Script** — caption spelling and spoken spelling side by side (numbers as words, stress marks/respellings for brand and domain).
- **Voice** — engine, voice, takes (2–3 seeds), language code, credit estimate (characters × takes), how the take is chosen.
- **Music** — tempo, key, instruments, where it drops and where it lands, level under VO (≥ 15 dB while words are spoken), final chord.
- **SFX plan** — per event type (title whoosh, landing hit, device move, tap, logo), library and licence.
- **Assets** — every external file with source and licence (device bezels, fonts, photos, logos from the official press kit / repo).
- **Avoid** — moves used in the previous ads of the batch.

## Standing rules
- Real brand: official logo files, real product UI captured from the product, facts checked on the live site/code. No illustrated placeholders.
- Photos: licensed stock only (Unsplash/Pexels), credited; ask before downloading paid assets.
- Look: dark stage with a slow brand-coloured light (brand colours, never purple/blue defaults), kinetic type, heavy but smooth motion blur, a thin motion-graphics layer (tap ripples, rings, counters) — new moves every ad.
- 60 fps; deliver 9:16 and 16:9 as **separate compositions** (9:16: type above, device below; 16:9: type left, device right).
- Voice: whole script in one request, 2 takes, respelled brand names; re-time picture to the voice.
- Music: original (code-made or properly licensed), arranged around the voice, ≥ 15 dB under it while speaking, no limiter squashing the voice; master −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
- Sound design dense and motivated: whoosh into titles, hit on landings, a sound on every device move, tap and logo.
- End card ~2 s after the last line, last chord ringing under it. Use the brand's end card (logo + domain), not someone else's signature.
- Order of work: brief → assets list → voice options → preview + contact sheet → finals (both ratios), stems (VO / music / SFX), README.
